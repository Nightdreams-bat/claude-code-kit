description: Interview me to pull what's in my head — remaining work, what I'm avoiding, what actually matters. Usage: /prompt-me [project or area]
---

The argument is: `$ARGUMENTS`

Interview the user to surface priorities and blockers that are in their head but not written
down. Scope to `$ARGUMENTS` if given, else the current project.

Ask **one pointed question at a time**, wait for the answer, then follow the thread. Aim at:

- What work is actually left — concretely, not the vague shape.
- What they're avoiding, and why.
- What genuinely matters here vs. what only feels urgent.
- What they've been treating as important that doesn't actually move anything.
- The decision or task that, if resolved, unblocks the most.

After ~5–8 exchanges, write it up: a short ranked "what matters / what's next" list, and note
anything worth saving to `context/` or the vault. Don't lecture — the value is what *they* say.
