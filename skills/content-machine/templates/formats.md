# formats.md - one section per output format; load the relevant one at draft time

## LinkedIn post
- 1 line hook, blank line, then short paragraphs (1-2 sentences).
- 900-1300 chars. One idea. End on a takeaway line, not a question, not "Agree?".
- No hashtag spam (0-3 max, at the end).

## X thread
- Tweet 1 = the whole point + a reason to keep reading. Standalone.
- 1 idea per tweet, <=270 chars. Numbers/specifics over adjectives.
- Last tweet = the payoff or a clean restatement. Optional soft CTA.

## X single / long post
- First 2 lines must work as the preview. Then the full argument.
- Tighter than a blog post; every sentence pulls the next.

## Newsletter / long post
- Title = the promise. First paragraph = why this matters now + what they'll get.
- Subheads every 150-250 words. One concrete example per section.
- Close with the single thing to remember.

## Video script (YouTube)
- Cold open: the payoff or the tension, first 10 seconds, no "hey guys".
- Then: context -> the meat in 3-5 beats -> recap -> one CTA.
- Written to be spoken: contractions, short clauses, direct address.

## Podcast promo
- The one moment/quote from the episode that makes someone click.
- 2-3 sentences + who it's for.
