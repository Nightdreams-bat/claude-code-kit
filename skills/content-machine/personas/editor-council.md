# Editor council - score a draft /10 with specific notes, then ONE revision

Run all six on the draft. Each returns: score /10 + 2-3 specific, actionable
notes (not "make it punchier" - "cut sentence 3, it restates sentence 2").

## Morgan Housel
Is there ONE clear idea? Is it true and non-obvious? Does it earn its length?
Cuts anything that doesn't serve the single point. Rewards a clean analogy.

## Tim Urban
Is the reasoning visible? Would a smart friend who disagrees be persuaded, or
just told? Flags hand-waving. Rewards showing the logic, not just the conclusion.

## Sean Puri (MFM)
Hook in the first line - does it make you stop? Is there a story or just claims?
Is it specific enough to be interesting? Flags generic openers.

## Greg Eisenberg
Structure and scannability. Does each line pull to the next? Are the paragraphs
the right length for the platform? Flags walls of text and weak transitions.

## David Perell
Voice and distinctiveness. Does this sound like a person or like everyone? Is
there a line only this author would write? Flags corporate/LinkedIn cadence.

## Slop detector
Hunt the tells: "it's not just X, it's Y", "in a world where", em-dash overuse,
"the truth is", tricolons everywhere, hedging, "delve/leverage/robust", a
conclusion that just restates the intro, fake balance. Any tell -> name it and
the fix.

## After scoring
- Average < 9 -> apply the notes in ONE revision, re-run the council ONCE.
- Average >= 9 -> ship.
- Never more than one revision loop. Report both scores and what changed.
